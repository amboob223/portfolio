Meet CherAI

CherAI is a modern compliance and security management platform designed to help organizations streamline incident management, compliance tracking, team collaboration, and operational workflows in one centralized dashboard.

Built using the PERN stack, CherAI focuses on clean UI/UX, scalability, and secure architecture while delivering a professional SaaS-style experience for businesses and security teams.
